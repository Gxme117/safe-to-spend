# Money tracker: notes for Claude Code

## What this is
A personal finance app whose first job is answering **"what can I spend today?"**.
Solo user (UK, Monzo), designed so a partner can be added later.

## How to think about the work: the Pareto principle
Before building anything, ask: what 20% of the effort gets 80% of the result?
- Pick the version of a feature that delivers most of its value for the least work, ship that, and only go further if it's clearly worth it.
- When suggesting or planning work, lead with the highest-leverage option and say what's being left out and why.
- Don't polish edge cases or build for hypotheticals before the core loop ("what can I spend today?") is solid.

## How it runs
- Published as a Claude artifact: one self-contained HTML file built from `src/` by `npm run build`.
- Runtime APIs come from the artifact host, not the browser:
  - `window.claude` database: per-user data under `data/users/me` (`state` doc, plus `tx-YYYY-MM` docs per month).
  - `mcp` host connector `host:monzo` (only in the Claude desktop app): `monzo_get_balance`, `monzo_list_pots`, `monzo_list_transactions`.
- No network requests to other sites, no remote images. Fonts: Google Fonts only.

## The money model
The rules are in the Pinch PRD (https://claude.ai/artifact/XCjmkQAaNCikgt6apYL1SX, v0.4: §10, §16, Appendix A). Keep this section and the PRD agreeing.
- Groups: **Bills** (fixed, dated), **Savings** (pots with role `save`), **Everyday** (a monthly budget per category the user chooses, not need/want by merchant), **Free** (the rest).
- Daily number = (spending money - unpaid bills before payday - what's left of everyday amounts - savings not moved yet this pay) / days to payday.
- Today-first (PRD T3, T3b): today's amount is set at the start of the day (free money then ÷ days left, `hero().perDay`). What you spend today comes off it straight away (`left`); past it, the days after come down. £30 a day, spend £30: £0 left today, tomorrow still £30. Spend £50: "From tomorrow, £27 a day".
- Spending money = main account + pots with role `spend`. Savings/business pots are never spending money.
- Savings (PRD #12, mobile only so far): you choose an amount a month (`state.saving`, Settings > Savings). Each pay cycle's share (`savingPerPay`: monthly × 12 over a year of paydays) is held back from the daily number until it's moved, counted net (`save` payments, and Monzo moves into a `save` pot, less moves back out, which come in as income with `cat: "savings"`). Moved, it's left the balance, so it isn't held twice. Only what bills and everyday leave over is held (`savingHeld`): savings give way and never cause a gap. No catch-up in later pays yet. Today shows "£X to put away this pay" (`SaveCard`): "I've moved it" without a bank (Monzo's sync notices by itself), "Not this time" (`saveKey`). Each future payday in the forecast holds its share too.
- When there isn't enough: bills, then everyday get what's there, and the daily number is £0, never below (Safe to Spend in the mobile app too, today and days to come). The line under it says how to close the gap, calmly. With money in savings pots: "Move £X from savings to cover what's coming". With none, or for what savings can't cover, how much more is needed in the way the user earns: hourly pay with a known take-home rate, "About N more hours of work covers your bills and everyday" (rounded up; the rate is `rate` on the main income, mobile engine only, nowhere to enter it in the app yet); else "You need £X more to cover your bills and everyday". Never say "short".
- Bills bigger than one pay (weekly pay, monthly rent; a yearly bill) are held back across the pays before them (amount ÷ pays left before it's due, PRD #28; mobile only so far, T12 passes). `billsAhead` in `lib/calc.ts`: each bill's next due date from payday on is shared over the pay cycles from the first payday after it last went out (or the cycle you started tracking in, or the bill's `since` if added later) to the one it's due in, counted up as pays go by (2 pays in, 2 shares held), so money held last pay isn't held twice. It's part of `billsLeft` (bills, before everyday and savings); the forecast sets aside one more share each payday. Monthly pay and monthly bills hold nothing ahead. Paid early, nothing held.
- Late pay (PRD #4, mobile only so far): when the last payday passed with no pay (not confirmed, not marked "not coming", counted from `since`) and the next one isn't due within a week, payday is assumed a week away (`late` on `Calc`, `latePay` in `lib/income.ts`). Snoozing doesn't end it. No forecast on the assumed payday, no pay row in Coming up, and the calendar and month grid don't mark it as pay.
- No Tight state for now (PRD #20 parked).
- Never count money that hasn't arrived. Ask instead of guessing (e.g. unsorted payments).
- Never scold. Spending big one day just lowers later days; no "overspending" language.

## Design rules
- Font: Manrope. Spacing on a 4pt grid (`--s1`..`--s7` = 4/8/12/16/24/32/48).
- Accent colour (`--btn-bg` / `--btn-ink`) means "money that's yours to use or coming in". Use it sparingly.
- Every number says why in one plain sentence. Notes under the big number stay on one line.
- Light and dark mode both matter. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- Don't recreate other companies' logos, card designs or brand colours.
- Picture and animation work (overlays, crops, a character's blink): measure from the real pixels, not by eye, and composite each result onto the real picture and look at it before calling it done; iterate until it's right. For organic motion, pre-render replacement poses offline the stop-motion way (like `mobile/scripts/tim_eyes.py`) and swap them in held poses, rather than faking it at runtime with clips and flat colours.

## Workflow
Edit `src/`, run `npm run build`, check `dist/money-tracker.html`, commit.
Publishing to the live link is done from the Claude chat.
Work in milestones: each ends in something you can try on the phone. Finish each step in it well (tests, typecheck, lint green) before the next, and get the milestone confirmed before starting the next one.

## Mobile app (`mobile/`)
An Expo (SDK 58, React Native 0.88) + React Native + TypeScript version of the core loop. It lives beside the web app; the web app stays the live one.
The money model and design rules above apply to both. For Expo itself, follow `mobile/AGENTS.md`: check the versioned docs, install with `npx expo install`. Expo Go on a real iPhone only runs the newest SDK, so the app has to stay on it. `mobile/.npmrc` sets `legacy-peer-deps` (datetimepicker lists react-native-windows as a peer). Refs to native views use React Native's instance types (`ScrollViewInstance`, `TextInputInstance`, `HostInstance`), not the component types.
- `src/lib/`: the money engine, pure TypeScript ported from `src/app.js`. Every function takes `{state, txs, today}`, never globals. Keep its results matching the web app's.
- `src/db/`: payments in SQLite (`tx` table, amounts in whole pence, migrations via `PRAGMA user_version`). Pence to pounds happens only in `db/transactions.ts`.
- `src/store/`: Zustand. The settings doc (same shape as the web `state` doc) persists through `expo-sqlite/kv-store`. Payments are written to SQLite before the store updates.
- `src/app/`: routes only (Expo Router). Tests never go in here, or they become routes.
- Blur: React Native's `filter: blur()` is behind an off-by-default flag on iOS, so it does nothing here. For a CSS `blur(Npx)`, draw the text with `react-native-svg` through `<FeGaussianBlur stdDeviation={N}>` (see `SoftAmount` in `components/Hero.tsx`). Don't fake it with stacked copies.
- Glass: the Safe to Spend card can be Liquid Glass (`expo-glass-effect`, `cardStyle` in the store, Settings > Appearance), its text in the theme's ink. The choice only shows on iOS 26 (`theme/glass.ts`); anywhere else, or on Colour (the default), it's the accent card. Never set `opacity: 0` on it or anything around it (the glass stops drawing), so the wallet moves cards, it doesn't fade them.
- Taps on text controls (like the balance eye) don't dim; only the thing that changes animates. The eye works on a bank card tucked behind too, without bringing it forward. Keep show/hide fades short (about .1s): a crossfade reads slower than the web's growing blur.
- Monzo (balance, pots and payments): a personal Monzo developer client, no server. Client ID/secret and tokens live in the Keychain (`expo-secure-store`, `src/services/monzo.ts`), never in the repo or app state. Login: Safari, then paste the `https://localhost/monzo?code=…` address back (Expo Go can't catch a fixed redirect, and Monzo's login refuses an `exp://` one). Approval in the Monzo app is noticed by itself. Start over keeps the login (it's in the Keychain): setup reads from it straight away; only Disconnect removes it. A sync re-anchors `balance` to main account + `spend` pots. New pots start as `spend`: setup shows the total of everything first (it can be connected from the first setup question), and you set savings pots aside after. Connected from setup (on the first question, or on "Want us to do the boring part?" just before the bills), it reads about 3 months of payments once (Monzo shares 90 days after the first 5 minutes) and suggests from them (`src/lib/found.ts`, all pure): pay (`findPays`: payers who repeat, pay-sized payments only, the most first; else the biggest `income` in 35 days and any about its size; none, no guess), payday (the pay rule that best fits: every payday it expects has a pay within 3 days, and it explains all pays but one; the reason says "around" when it isn't exact), regular payments (`findBills`: the same payee at least twice on a rule that fits, still going; direct debits at any amount, card payments and transfers only when about the same each month or 4 weeks; food and drink and weekly card spending never) and everyday spending (`findSpending`: card payments by Monzo category a month, refunds off, found bills left out). Found isn't confirmed: nothing is saved until you've seen it. Pay is asked, not assumed ("Yes, that's right", "Choose another", "I'll enter it myself"); confirmed, it's what lands each payday and the income keeps `payer`, so a synced pay from them counts as payday. Nothing found: the page is as before. Another bank provider would plug in by returning the same `SetupFound` to `setSetupBank`. New payments are imported too, and the Day tab syncs quietly on open if the last sync is over 15 minutes old. A payment out that paid a bill is filed against it as it comes in (`billMatch` in `lib/monzo.ts`, Monzo and linked banks alike), so it's off what's held back and isn't spending as well: paid to the bill's `payee` (or every word of its name is in the payment's, so "Netflix" finds "NETFLIX.COM" but "Rent" not "RENTOKIL"), within 10% of its amount (half to double if it varies), and near a due date the bank hasn't paid yet (a week early to 3 days late, `nearDue`, `billSlot`). The amount alone never matches; two bills that fit one payment means neither is filed, and it's asked. Each sync also files older unsorted ones (`unfiledBills`), and a bill payment you logged by hand goes once the bank's covers the same due date (`standIns`). Filing a bank payment under a bill with no payee teaches it the payee. Payments keep the shop logo Monzo sends (an https link, loaded from Monzo's servers; the web app's no-remote-images rule still stands). No logo: a line icon for what it probably was, on a soft colour (`lib/looks.ts` picks it, `components/LookIcon.tsx` draws it: by category, else guessed from the name; bills by name; pay and money in their own; colours `badgeTint` in `theme/tokens.ts`). Pure logic is in `src/lib/monzo.ts`.
- Other banks (TrueLayer, Open Banking; sandbox for now): `supabase/functions/bank/` is the only place the client secret is used (Supabase secrets `TL_CLIENT_ID`, `TL_CLIENT_SECRET`, `TL_ENV`; never in the repo or the app). It stores nothing and reads no bank data: `/start` opens TrueLayer's login, `/callback` hands the one-time code back to the app (only `moneytracker://` or `exp://`), `/token` and `/refresh` swap it for tokens. The app reads balances and payments from TrueLayer itself with the token. Pure parts in `lib.ts`, `deno test` there. No sign-in on it yet: needed before anyone else uses it. In the app, "Add an account" (Today's cards, Settings) opens `app/link-bank.tsx`: the login runs in `expo-web-browser`'s auth session, which closes by itself when the function sends the code back (`Linking.createURL("link-bank")`), then you say which of its accounts are spending money. `services/truelayer.ts` holds each link's tokens in the Keychain (`tl.<linkId>.*`) and refreshes them through the function; `lib/truelayer.ts` (pure) maps TrueLayer into the shapes Monzo uses: payments to `RawTx` (ids `tl:…`, no category, so they're asked), and the bank to a `Bank` in `state.links` (main = the first current account at its `current` balance, never `available`; other accounts as pots, savings set aside at first). Each linked bank's card on Today shows everything in it, in a colour of our own; the Monzo card has the rest.
